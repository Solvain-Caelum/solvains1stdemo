# Solvains1stdemo

Create a premium, modern, mobile-responsive website for a fictional fitness centre called Titan Fitness.
Design:
Dark charcoal background, white typography and lime-green accents.
Professional fitness photography.
Clean typography, generous spacing and smooth animations.
Sections:
Navigation with logo and links.
Hero section with a strong headline and a “Join Now” button.
Training programmes and benefits.
Membership plans with clear prices.
Trainer profiles.
Location, opening hours and contact details.
Footer with social links.
Requirements:
Make the layout look excellent on desktop and mobile.
Make all navigation links and buttons work.
Make “Join Now” open a contact or enquiry section.
Use reusable components and consistent styling.
Do not invent real testimonials, awards or business claims.
Use placeholder business details clearly marked for replacement.
Do not implement fake payments or booking functionality.
The result should look like a professional website that a real local gym could customize for its business.
